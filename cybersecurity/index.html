Cybersecurity

Practical cybersecurity for the environment you actually operate.

Cybersecurity is no longer an endpoint problem. Modern threats move through identities, email, cloud applications, endpoints, vendors, remote access, and human behavior. Protecting the business requires controls that work together across the entire technology environment.

Frontline combines cybersecurity strategy, modern security platforms, continuous monitoring, and hands-on technical expertise to help smaller organizations reduce risk, strengthen resilience, and improve their ability to respond when something goes wrong — without a security team of their own.

Protect the identity. Protect the cloud. Protect the endpoint.

The traditional office perimeter is gone.

Employees now work across cloud applications, SaaS platforms, mobile devices, home networks, and multiple locations. An attacker may never need to breach a firewall if they can compromise a user’s identity instead.

  • Identity

    Access security, multi-factor authentication, identity threat detection and response, and the access-management processes behind them.

  • Endpoint

    Endpoint protection, managed detection and response, device management, and security configuration and hardening.

  • Cloud

    Microsoft 365 and cloud security, SaaS data protection, email security controls, and secure configuration baselines.

  • Monitoring

    24/7 Security Operations Center monitoring, alert triage, and the ability to detect, understand, and contain activity that matters.

  • Recovery

    Backup and recovery, SaaS backup, and incident readiness planning so the business can come back from a bad day.

  • People

    Security awareness training, phishing awareness and testing, and policy and security control development.

Frontline builds security around the way modern organizations actually operate, combining endpoint, identity, cloud, access, monitoring, and recovery controls into a more complete security program.

24/7 monitoring and response

Security events do not follow business hours.

Frontline leverages managed detection and response capabilities backed by around-the-clock security operations to identify suspicious activity and help respond when something is wrong.

The objective is not simply to generate more alerts. It is to improve the organization’s ability to detect, understand, contain, and respond to threats.

Your employees are part of the security program

Technology alone cannot stop every attack.

Frontline can provide ongoing security awareness training that helps employees recognize phishing, social engineering, credential theft, unsafe data handling, and emerging threats.

As AI-powered phishing, impersonation, and social engineering become more convincing, keeping employees informed becomes even more important.

Security awareness should not be treated as a once-a-year checkbox. It should reinforce better security habits throughout the organization.

Cybersecurity and compliance should reinforce one another

Not simply a collection of security products.

Security controls should not exist solely because a checklist requires them.

Frontline connects cybersecurity improvements to governance frameworks, organizational policies, risk assessments, compliance requirements, cyber-insurance requirements, and broader business priorities.

That creates a security program leadership can understand, measure, and defend.

Frontline can begin with an assessment of your current security posture and identify the areas presenting the greatest risk. From there, we help build a prioritized remediation plan based on business impact, technical risk, and organizational priorities.

That may include strengthening identity controls, improving Microsoft 365 security, deploying or reconfiguring security platforms, remediating vulnerabilities, improving backup and recovery, hardening infrastructure, or implementing the controls required by a compliance or insurance initiative.
