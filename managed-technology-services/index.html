Managed
Technology Services

Technology services that carry strategy into execution.

A technology plan is only useful if someone can implement it, operate it, document it, and keep it working.

Frontline provides managed technology services backed by experienced engineers across cloud, cybersecurity, infrastructure, networking, systems, and modern IT operations.

Talk With Frontline Relationship models →

We can operate as your primary technology provider, supplement an internal IT team, work alongside another service provider, or take responsibility for specific parts of the environment.

More than a help desk

Managed technology should do more than react to tickets.

Frontline helps organizations build technology environments that are standardized, documented, secure, supportable, and aligned with the direction of the business.

Services can include:

  • End-user technology support
  • Endpoint management
  • Microsoft 365 administration
  • Cloud services
  • Identity and access management
  • Infrastructure and network support
  • Cybersecurity management
  • SaaS backup and protection
  • Documentation and runbooks
  • Vendor management
  • Technology lifecycle planning
  • Onboarding and offboarding
  • Licensing and procurement
  • Projects and migrations
  • Security remediation
  • Infrastructure modernization

Standardization

Standardize before complexity becomes the standard.

Growing organizations often inherit years of one-off technology decisions. Different vendors. Different configurations. Undocumented systems. Aging equipment. Inconsistent security. Critical knowledge that lives with one employee or service provider.

Frontline helps bring structure to those environments through:

  • Standardized configurations
  • Centralized documentation
  • Defined technology standards
  • Repeatable onboarding and offboarding
  • Access-management procedures
  • Lifecycle planning
  • Security baselines
  • Vendor accountability
  • Operational runbooks

The goal is an environment that is easier to support, easier to secure, and easier to manage.

Advisory, co-managed, or fully managed

Frontline does not require every client relationship to look the same.

  • Fully Managed Technology

    Frontline takes primary responsibility for the technology environment, including support, administration, security, documentation, and ongoing improvement.

  • Co-Managed IT

    Frontline supplements internal technology staff with engineering, cybersecurity, documentation, projects, governance, or specialized expertise.

  • Strategic + Technical Support

    Frontline provides Fractional CIO or governance leadership while supporting selected areas of the technology environment.

  • Projects & Remediation

    Frontline can be engaged for a defined technology initiative without replacing the client’s existing IT team or service provider.

Strategy backed by engineers

That continuity matters.

Frontline combines technology leadership with hands-on implementation capability. When a Fractional CIO roadmap, GRC assessment, cybersecurity review, or business initiative identifies work that needs to happen, our engineers can help carry that work through.

The people establishing priorities understand the business objective, and the people implementing the solution understand why the work is being performed.

Built for organizations that need more structure

When technology requires greater planning, documentation, security, and accountability.

Frontline is particularly well suited to small and midsized organizations that have reached that point.

That may include organizations with:

  • No internal IT department
  • A small internal IT team
  • An existing MSP
  • Rapid growth
  • Regulatory requirements
  • Cybersecurity concerns
  • Multiple locations
  • Complex cloud environments
  • Aging infrastructure
  • Upcoming mergers, acquisitions, or transitions
